Whitebored

A Mac tutor that sits on the table with you. You write on paper or a whiteboard. You talk. It looks, listens, draws on its own board, and speaks.



https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea95cde2-98de-462a-816e-ea32bcb47049



What it does
- You type one line: what you want to learn.
- It uses the camera, the mic, and its own whiteboard.
- It stays silent until you press enter.
- Then it takes a photo, grabs what you just said, shows thinking, writes a clear answer, and speaks it. It helps you learn.
